Why use it?

Build a résumé that remembers more than job titles.

Most people forget valuable work they've done. Record a project, describe what you accomplished, and save evidence if you have it. Your Work Passport organizes that experience so you can use it when preparing a résumé, applying for a position, or explaining your skills.

1. Record your work

2. Show your skills

3. Build your résumé

Turn your experience into clear accomplishments you can copy into a résumé.

Trust model

What “verified” means here

This prototype demonstrates the workflow. In the real product, a record should become official only after the evidence is authenticated by an approved verification method.

Core rule: if it is in the official Work Passport, it is verified.
Control

You decide what becomes part of the Passport

Evidence can stay private, be rejected, or be verified and added. The Passport is meant to be portable without turning unverified statements into official credentials.

Evidence

Work records are tied to proof instead of standing as unsupported claims.
